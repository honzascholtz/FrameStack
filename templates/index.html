Welcome to FrameStack

FrameStack is a tool for plotting and comparing bicycle geometries to help you find the right frame size and stem/spacer combinations.

How to use:

  • Paste your bike geometry measurements (tab-separated) directly into the text boxes on the left.
  • Importing from Geometry Geeks: Go to the bike's page on Geometry Geeks, click and drag to highlight their geometry table, copy it (Ctrl+C), and paste it directly into the text box here!
  • Alternatively, use the file browser to load a .txt or .csv file directly.
  • Click + Add Another Bike to compare multiple frames overlaid on top of each other.
  • Click Plot Comparison to generate a detailed 2D side-view diagram with key measurements annotated.
